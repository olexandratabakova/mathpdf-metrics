You are an expert in the subject matter of these flashcards. Rate the difficulty of each card for a student who is seeing the material for the first time.

You will receive a numbered list of flashcard FRONTS (questions only), in order.

For each card, assign a difficulty rating from 1 to 5:
  1 — Very easy: basic recall or trivial definition
  2 — Easy: straightforward concept, minimal reasoning needed
  3 — Medium: requires understanding, not just recall
  4 — Hard: requires connecting multiple concepts or non-obvious reasoning
  5 — Very hard: requires deep insight, proof construction, or complex application

Return a JSON object with:
{
  "ratings": [
    {"card_number": <int>, "difficulty": <1-5>},
    ...
  ]
}

List all cards in the same order they were given.

The cards may be in Ukrainian or English.
